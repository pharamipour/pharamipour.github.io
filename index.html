About

The Artist
Behind the Worlds

P. Haramipour is a concept artist and illustrator specializing in the dark, the mythic, and the strange. Working across the full spectrum of speculative fiction — from intimate character studies to vast environmental canvases — each piece is built to evoke a complete world.

With roots in architecture and a deep fascination with non-human anatomy, creature behavior, and environmental storytelling, the work synthesizes technical precision with visceral imagination.

Available for game development, publishing, entertainment, and independent commissions.

Areas of Practice

Environment & World Design

Atmospheric environments that establish tone, lore, and scale. From ritual grounds to alien ecosystems — spaces that feel lived-in and mythically charged.

🐉

Creature Design

Anatomically coherent fantasy fauna — predators, guardians, and aberrations built from biological logic and visual impact. Every creature has a story in its silhouette.

⚔️

Character Design

Iconic figures that carry culture, history, and power in every detail. From warrior queens to urban mythologies — characters who own their world.

Narrative Illustration

Single-image stories — cover art, key moments, and symbolic compositions designed to stop the viewer and pull them in. Light, composition, drama.
